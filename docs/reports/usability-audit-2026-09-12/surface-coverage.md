## Reading the matrix

- **Partial** means only the explicitly recorded states/actions were exercised. **Smoke only** means a route loaded; its controls and data have not been audited. **Source only** means no native interaction evidence is recorded. **Deferred remainder** records the user's 12 September direction to defer deeper MCP, Worktrees, Launcher, Import, Compare and Replay exploration while preserving any completed basic checks and outstanding verification of bounded fixes.
- D = 1440×960, M = 960×640, L = 2560×1440 CSS pixels. `P` means partial coverage at that measured size; `—` means no attributable check at that size. An observed interaction with unrecorded dimensions does not fill a viewport cell. No row currently asserts every state passed at any size.
- A route row and its nested rows describe different levels of coverage. Opening the route does not complete its nested workflows. Global sizing/Settings shell smoke does not establish that every Settings control was used.
- Evidence codes refer to the catalog below. Private artifacts remain local and ignored under `.playwright-cli`. Six fixture-only toolbar/Analytics/Code copies passed root privacy review and are linked from findings under `evidence/`; those are the only approved public screenshot subset at this checkpoint. Filenames are audit references, not published evidence links. Only screenshots explicitly recorded as inspected by the live owner support visual conclusions. HMR interrupted some panel captures; those remain partial and need stable recapture.
- Pending work includes keyboard/focus behavior, loading/empty/error/disabled/success variants, both themes, supported scale settings, and D/M/L wherever applicable, in addition to each row's specific next steps. These cross-cutting checks are not silently waived for rows that name only a narrower next step; explicitly deferred deep workflows remain unverified.
