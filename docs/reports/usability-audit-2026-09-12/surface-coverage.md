## Reading the matrix

- **Partial** means only the explicitly recorded states/actions were exercised. **Smoke only** means a route loaded; its controls and data have not been audited. **Source only** means no native interaction evidence is recorded. **Deferred remainder** records the user's 12 September direction to defer deeper MCP, Worktrees, Launcher, Import, Compare, Replay, SDK and Alerts exploration while preserving completed basic checks and each fix's explicit verification limits.
- D = 1440×960, M = 960×640, L = 2560×1440 CSS pixels. `P` means partial coverage at that measured size; `—` means no attributable check at that size. An observed interaction with unrecorded dimensions does not fill a viewport cell. No row currently asserts every state passed at any size.
- A route row and its nested rows describe different levels of coverage. Opening the route does not complete its nested workflows. Global sizing/Settings shell smoke does not establish that every Settings control was used.
- Evidence codes refer to the catalog below. Private artifacts remain local and ignored under `.playwright-cli`. Six fixture-only toolbar/Analytics/Code copies passed root privacy review and are linked from findings under `evidence/`; those are the only approved public screenshot subset at this checkpoint. Filenames are audit references, not published evidence links. Only screenshots explicitly recorded as inspected by the live owner support visual conclusions. HMR interrupted some captures; those are excluded from stable visual proof unless a later settled check is explicitly recorded.
- Unexercised variants include some keyboard/focus, loading/empty/error/disabled/success, theme/scale and D/M/L combinations. These are explicit residual limits of the bounded audit, not native passes. Each row identifies its observed subset and unverified remainder; no remaining variant is silently promoted to complete or promised as automatic follow-up.
