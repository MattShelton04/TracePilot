### Fixed

- **Session events in the conversation** — Compact and Timeline now show events logged before a turn, such as "Session started", above its prompt instead of after its reply. In all three views, events logged while a turn ran, such as a rejected tool call or an interruption, now follow that turn's tool calls instead of preceding them.
- **Recent sessions kept ready** — The setting now saves when you leave the field or press Enter, and then shows the saved number. Clearing the field or pressing Escape keeps the saved value. Typing "-3" no longer saves 100, and the field no longer shows a number other than the saved one.
